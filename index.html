The Ritual

Prepare your bowl

◆   ◆   ◆

Matcha is more than a drink — it is a moment of presence. Follow these steps to create the perfect bowl.

"The preparation of matcha is a form of moving meditation. Each gesture — the sifting, the pouring, the whisking — is deliberate and unhurried. In this ritual, you are not merely making tea. You are making time."

茶之道
01

Sift

Pass 1–2 tsp of matcha through a fine sieve into a warm bowl to remove clumps.

02

Add Water

Pour 70°C water — about 80ml for usucha. Never use boiling water.

03

Whisk

Whisk in a gentle "W" motion with a bamboo chasen until a fine foam forms on the surface.

04

Enjoy

Drink from the bowl. Notice the sweetness, the umami, the quiet moment you have created.

Our Origin

From Uji, Kyoto

◆

Uji has been considered the home of Japan's finest matcha for over 800 years. The region's cool climate, morning mist, and rich soil create conditions that cannot be replicated elsewhere.

◆

Leaves shaded for 25–30 days before harvest, boosting L-theanine and chlorophyll concentration.

◆

Stone-ground at 30 grams per hour on granite mills — slow enough to preserve full flavor and color.

◆

Direct relationship with family farms across three generations. No intermediaries.

◆

Every batch carries a batch code with full traceability from field to bowl.
