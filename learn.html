Preparation

Step-by-Step Guides

◆
薄茶 Usucha Whisked thin matcha — everyday ritual

Traditional

Usucha Preparation

The most common way to enjoy ceremonial matcha. Light, frothy, and aromatic.

  1. Warm your bowl with hot water, then dry it
  2. Sift 1–2 tsp (2g) of matcha into the bowl
  3. Add 80ml of water at 70°C (160°F)
  4. Whisk in a rapid "W" motion until frothy
  5. Drink directly from the bowl, savoring each sip
濃茶 Koicha Thick ceremonial tea — special occasions

Ceremonial

Koicha Preparation

A concentrated, velvety preparation reserved for the highest grade matcha and special ceremonies.

  1. Warm and dry your bowl as with usucha
  2. Sift 3–4 tsp (4g) of ceremonial matcha
  3. Add only 30ml of water at 70°C (160°F)
  4. Knead slowly in a circular motion — no frothing
  5. Drink in three slow, mindful sips
焙じ茶の淹れ方 Hou Ji Cha Steeped roasted green tea — everyday ease

Roasted Tea

Hou Ji Cha Preparation

Unlike matcha, Hou Ji Cha is steeped like a traditional tea. Simple, accessible, and deeply satisfying.

  1. Use 3g of Hou Ji Cha per 200ml of water
  2. Heat water to 85°C (185°F) — not boiling
  3. Steep for 60 seconds
  4. Strain and enjoy hot or over ice
  5. Can be re-steeped 2–3 times

Matcha Toolkit

You don't need much to begin your matcha journey. Here are the essentials:

  • ◆ Bamboo whisk (chasen) — creates the foam
  • ◆ Bamboo scoop (chashaku) — measures portions
  • ◆ Ceramic bowl (chawan) — holds the tea
  • ◆ Fine sieve — prevents clumps
  • ◆ Thermometer — perfect water temperature
